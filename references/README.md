## Observed Structure of `EJBL_BIGASAN.xlsx` (from Nov 20, 2026 review)
- **SALES:** Daily rice sales (`DATE`, `RICE TYPE`, `QTY`, `UNIT` [SACK/KG], `QTY (KG)/ISSUED`, `PRICE/KG`, `TOTAL`, `PICK UP/DELIVER`, `(EOD) TOTAL SALES`) + Price Guide (wholesale/retail per sack & per kg, walk-in vs. delivery tier notice: 1–2 sacks vs. 3+ sacks) + Consignment notes (`GA-RA-HE`, `CARIBBEAN GRILL`).
- **CASHBOOK:** Sales invoice log (`DATE`, `SALES INVOICE NO.`, `PARTICULARS`, `CASH SALES`, `ONLINE PAYMENT`, `TOTAL AMOUNT`, `NOTE` [GCash/BPI/InstaPay ref nos.], `CASH BOX`, `GCASH`, `TOTAL MONEY`, `SOBRA`/`SHORT`). Continuous SI series (`2607B0200201–250`, then `1–139`), continuous no-receipt series (`NSI-000–175`), owner contribution (`OWN-001`), multi-item invoices, delivery fees as separate line items, and split cash+online payments.
- **INVENTORY:** 22 rice types with codes (`CJ`, `CP`, `PH`, `JR`, `GS`, `SH`, `RS`, `S`, `JD`, `MC`, `DR`, `MD`, `AM`, `MB`, `MCL`, `SCB`, `SJR`, `O`, `BP`, `SA`, `PT`, `MO`), beginning/received/issued/ending kg, `UNIT COST/PUHUNAN (/KG)`, sacks, and ordering points.
- **EXPENSES:** Categorized disbursements (`OFFICE SUPPLIES`, `PERMIT`, `EQUIPMENT`, `SAFETY EQUIPMENT`, `TRANSPORTATION`, `REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE`, `SEALING AND LICENSING`, `OPEX-FUEL`, `OPEX-MARKETING`, `COGS` rice restocks, `PERSONAL WITHDRAWALS` `DRAW-001`–`DRAW-023`), payment method (`CASH`, `GCASH`, `ONLINE PAYMENT (BPI)`), status (`PAID`, with one margin note `not pait`).
- **CAPITAL:** Opening owner capital entry (`05/03/2026`: Debit `CASH` 80,000 / Credit `CAPITAL` 80,000).
